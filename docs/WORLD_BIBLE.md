# WORLD BIBLE

**Status:** CANON

## Premise
A Zombie LitRPG apocalypse unfolds across a dense modern city divided into districts.

## Interface
The Interface exists inside the world. Characters perceive it as a real phenomenon, not as a conventional game menu.

## Human baseline
For every base attribute, **1.00 = average healthy adult human baseline**.

## Skills
A character becomes what they repeatedly do. Skills improve through meaningful practice rather than generic level points.

## Abilities
Rare survivors awaken unique abilities. Ability branches emerge through actual usage patterns.

## Fragments
High-level infected and ability carriers may leave fragments or spheres. Absorbing a foreign ability can transfer memories, habits, emotional imprints or psychological distortions.

## Content lifecycle
`DRAFT -> REVIEW -> CANON`

Only approved CANON content may be treated as immutable world truth.
